Chang Thai — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

Developer / publisher: Aung Thaw Tar

Privacy contact: bangkokboss231@gmail.com

About Chang Thai

Chang Thai is a Thai-language learning app for Burmese speakers. Core lessons, phrase search, favorites, quizzes and learning progress can be used without creating an account.

Information stored on your device

The app stores learning preferences and progress locally, including favorites, recent phrases, theme preference, polite-form preference, learned phrase identifiers, quiz completion count, learning progress, audio volume and phrase-correction drafts. These local records are not uploaded to the Chang Thai project backend by the core learning and search features. On the web version, local browser storage is used. Clearing app/browser data or changing devices may remove or leave behind those local records.

Optional Weekly League and Google sign-in

If you choose to use Weekly League, the app uses Google sign-in and requests basic identity scopes (openid, email and profile). The app does not receive your Google password. Supabase Auth processes the sign-in identity. The League service stores account identity, your selected public alias and avatar, membership, submitted challenge answers and competitive scoring records. The leaderboard displays alias, avatar, rank and points; it does not display your email address or authentication UUID.

Weekly League is optional. Core offline learning does not require League sign-in.

Account deletion

You can request deletion from the signed-in League screen or use the external instructions at Chang Thai account deletion. The deletion process removes the League account and associated League profile, memberships, rounds, credits, completions and submitted answer records as implemented by the service. Local learning progress stored on your device is separate and is not deleted by League account deletion.

Notifications

If you enable announcements and grant Android notification permission when requested, Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to subscribe the device to Chang Thai announcement notifications. Firebase may process the messaging registration token and related delivery information to provide this service. You can disable notifications in the app settings and/or Android settings.

Advertising

Chang Thai includes Google Mobile Ads support. Ads are enabled in the public release. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, IP/network information and diagnostics as described in Google's current SDK disclosures and the applicable consent choices. Personalized advertising follows Google's consent framework where applicable, and users can manage ad privacy choices in the app Settings where available. Review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy guidance.

Text-to-speech

Thai pronunciation playback uses the operating system or browser speech engine. The voice provider and its processing may vary by device and user settings. Chang Thai does not upload a separate voice recording through its text-to-speech feature.

Search and phrase corrections

Core phrase search uses bundled app content. Search terms are not sent to the Chang Thai project backend by the core search feature. Phrase correction drafts are stored locally; they are shared with another party only if you copy and send them using another app or service.

Service providers and sharing

Depending on the features you choose, the app uses Google sign-in, Supabase services for optional League, Firebase Cloud Messaging for optional announcements, and Google Mobile Ads when advertising is enabled. These providers process information needed to provide their respective services under their own terms and privacy disclosures. Public League alias/avatar/score information is visible on the leaderboard.

Data retention and security

Local learning records remain on your device/browser until removed by you or by device/browser data management. League account data is deleted through the account deletion process described above. Supabase and Firebase retain service backups and logs according to their own standard retention practices; League data deleted through the account deletion process is removed from active systems, and remaining copies in provider backups expire with those backups. Online service requests use HTTPS where configured. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

Children

Chang Thai is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 18. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, SDKs or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy-policy URL shown on the Google Play listing.
